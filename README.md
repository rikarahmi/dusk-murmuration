**Dusk Murmuration** — guide a flock of starlings across a dusk sky and keep them alive until dawn. Steer the lead bird with the mouse (or arrow keys / WASD); the flock follows on springs, flowing and bending like a real murmuration. Collect glowing light motes to score, grow your combo (up to x5) and add birds to the flock. Storm clouds drift across the sky — one touch costs 3 birds and resets your combo — and periodic wind gusts push the whole flock sideways, announced a moment before they arrive. Survive the 90-second night to reach the dawn and win; lose every bird and the flock is gone.

Controls: mouse / arrows / WASD to steer · P pause · M mute · R restart · Enter or click to start. Sound is fully synthesised in WebAudio — chimes that climb with your combo, a low storm thud, a dawn chord — with no audio files and no network requests. The game is plain static files under `game/`: open `index.html` in any desktop browser and play offline.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser
```
